Edenva is more than ziplines. The moment you walk in, you feel the shift: mountains, river and jungle all around, with facilities built for spending the whole day even if you never get on a single line.

Here's some of what you'll find walking around the park:

- **Hammocks and rest areas** by the river, perfect for unwinding between activities.
- **Trails** through the vegetation of the Mismaloya mountains.
- **Safe river spots** to get in the water and cool off.
- **A restaurant** with a view of nature, so you can eat without leaving the park.
